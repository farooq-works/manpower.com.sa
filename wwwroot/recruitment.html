Our Screening & Hiring Process

We follow a step-by-step process to find the best candidates for your organization.

Step 1: Understanding Your Needs

We begin by getting to know your business, understanding your culture and values, and identifying the specific skills and qualities you’re looking for. Don’t worry if you don’t have answers to these questions. Our experts will offer you suggestions and help you determine the skills that could benefit your organization.

Step 2: Sourcing Candidates

Our expert team will tap into our extensive network and search for candidates who meet your criteria, both locally and globally.

Step 3: Screening

All the candidates will undergo an initial screening where their qualifications, experience, and values are evaluated. Those who pass this step are invited for interviews and skill assessments to further narrow down the candidate pool.

Step 4: Candidate Presentation

We will present you with a curated list of top candidates, complete with their detailed profiles and our expert recommendations.

Step 5: Decide On The Best Candidate

You get to make the final decision on who you want to hire. Our team will provide support and guidance throughout the negotiation and onboarding process.
